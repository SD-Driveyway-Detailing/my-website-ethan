Exterior Standard Wash

  • Paint-safe rinse using 1800 PSI and a wide 40° nozzle.
  • Foam cannon soap application to loosen dirt before contact.
  • Gentle microfiber hand wash for tougher spots.
  • Wheels, tires, windows, and mirrors cleaned thoroughly.
  • Microfiber hand dry to reduce streaks and water spots.
  • Tire shine applied for a clean final look.

Interior Express Clean

  • Trash pickup and removal.
  • Vacuum seats, floors, carpets, mats, and trunk.
  • Interior windows cleaned for a clear finish.
  • Quick wipe-down of dashboard, console, cupholders, and door panels.
  • Floor mats shaken or vacuumed and placed back neatly.
