Exterior

  • Ultra paint safe pressure wash (1800 PSI) with a wide (40°) spray nozzle removes loose dirt and cools the paint before washing begins.
  • Foam cannon applies thick foam breaking down the grime to be easily and safely washed off while serving as a protective barrier on the paint to guarantee paint safety.
  • Hand wash for tougher spots.
  • Windows, Mirrors, Rims, Tires cleaned thoroughly.
  • Microfiber hand dry for a streak-free finish.
  • Tire shine applied for a clean final look.

Interior

  • Trash pickup and removal.
  • Vacuum floors and trunk.
  • Vacuum carpets and floor mats.
  • Interior windows cleaned for a clear finish.
